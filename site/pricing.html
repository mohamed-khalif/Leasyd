Questions

What exactly am I paying for?

Two things per record you send (spans, log records and metric data points, counted per million): ingest, for receiving and processing it, and storage, for keeping it searchable for 30 days. Synthetic check runs are counted per thousand. Search, dashboards, alerts and team members are all included.

What are drop rules?

Rules you set in Leasyd to discard data as it arrives: debug logs, health-check spans, a noisy metric, or all but a sample (e.g. keep 10% of fast, successful traces, whole traces at a time). Dropped records are charged for ingest only and never stored, so they cost about a tenth of what you keep. Each rule shows what it would have dropped in the last 24 hours, and what that saves, before you turn it on.

How does the free trial work?

Sign up with just your email: no credit card. You get 7 days with every feature and up to 1 GB of data a day.

When the 7 days are up, Leasyd stops accepting new data and your checks pause, and you're never charged. Your data stays searchable until it ages out. To keep going, add payment details in Leasyd (Settings › Billing), during or after the trial: your account switches to pay as you go, with no upfront charge.

How am I billed?

Monthly, in US dollars, by card through Stripe. Each invoice counts what you used that month: records received, records stored, and check runs, at the prices above. Invoices and receipts are in Settings › Billing › Manage billing, where you can also change your card or cancel at any time.

Are there plans or tiers?

No. Everyone pays the same prices and gets the same product. You don't have to pick a plan or upgrade to unlock a feature.

Do you charge per user?

No. Invite your whole team at no extra cost.

How long is my data kept?

30 days for everything, included in the price. Older data is deleted automatically each day.

Is searching really included?

Yes. To keep Leasyd fast for everyone, each account has a daily search allowance that normal use doesn't come near. You can see how much you've used in Settings. If your team searches very heavily, talk to us and we'll raise it.

How do I keep my bill under control?

Your usage is visible at any time on the Usage & Cost page, broken down by service. We can also set a daily data limit on your account: anything over it is refused until midnight UTC, so a noisy service can't surprise you.

The biggest saving is in what you keep: add drop rules for debug logs and health checks, or keep a sample of busy traces. No changes to your apps needed.

What counts as one record?

One span is one step of a traced request. One log record is one log line or event. One metric data point is one value of one series at one moment. For example, a CPU gauge reported every 30 seconds sends about 86,000 points a month.

Where is my data stored?

In AWS, in the US East (N. Virginia) region, encrypted at rest and in transit. Each account's data sits in its own space, and AWS permissions stop any other account from reading it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There are no contracts. You pay for what you sent up to the day you stop.
