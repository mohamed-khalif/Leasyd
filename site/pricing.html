Questions

What exactly am I paying for?

Only the records you send: spans, log records and metric data points, counted per million, plus synthetic check runs, counted per thousand. Storage, search, dashboards, alerts and team members are all included.

How does the free trial work?

Sign up with just your email: no credit card. You get 7 days with every feature and up to 1 GB of data a day.

When the 7 days are up, Leasyd stops accepting new data and your checks pause, and you're never charged. Your data stays searchable until it ages out. To keep going, email mkhalif@leasyd.com and we'll switch your account to pay as you go.

Are there plans or tiers?

No. Everyone pays the same prices and gets the same product. You don't have to pick a plan or upgrade to unlock a feature.

Do you charge per user?

No. Invite your whole team at no extra cost.

How long is my data kept?

30 days for everything, included in the price. Older data is deleted automatically each day.

Is searching really included?

Yes. To keep Leasyd fast for everyone, each account has a daily search allowance that normal use doesn't come near. You can see how much you've used in Settings. If your team searches very heavily, talk to us and we'll raise it.

How do I keep my bill under control?

Your usage is visible at any time on the Usage & Cost page, broken down by service. We can also set a daily data limit on your account: anything over it is refused until midnight UTC, so a noisy service can't surprise you.

The biggest saving usually comes from sending less: drop debug logs in production, or sample busy traces in your OpenTelemetry Collector.

What counts as one record?

One span is one step of a traced request. One log record is one log line or event. One metric data point is one value of one series at one moment. For example, a CPU gauge reported every 30 seconds sends about 86,000 points a month.

Where is my data stored?

In AWS, in the US East (N. Virginia) region, encrypted at rest and in transit. Each account's data sits in its own space, and AWS permissions stop any other account from reading it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There are no contracts. You pay for what you sent up to the day you stop.
