  3. Point OpenTelemetry at LeasydSet these in any OpenTelemetry SDK or Collector: 
    OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT=https://ingest.leasyd.com
OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_HEADERS=x-api-key=<your key>
